# Last Bus — Art Direction v2

The detailed visual direction is a gritty dieselpunk post-apocalyptic road-survival world: battered vehicles, improvised armor, rust, dust, welded repairs, and distinct survivor silhouettes.

## Palette
- Charcoal and near-black for outlines and deep shadows.
- Dusty ochre and sand for sun-bleached paint, roads, and concrete.
- Rust orange and burnt sienna for corrosion and selective warm highlights.
- Muted olive / military green for vehicles and equipment.
- Desaturated steel blue for glass reflections and cold shadows.

## Rendering rules
- Dense but readable silhouettes with crisp dark outlines.
- Layered material detail: chipped paint, rust blooms, weld seams, bolts, patch plates, grime, cracked glass, and dust.
- Keep highlights selective; avoid clean modern surfaces and generic smooth gradients.
- Give characters distinct silhouettes and equipment profiles.

## Asset pack
A downloadable concept pack accompanies this direction. It contains separate PNG crops for the player bus, raider bus, survivors, weapons and gear, settlements, wasteland terrain, and camp / road props.

The PNGs are detailed concept crops, not final production sprite sheets. Before runtime integration, slice individual objects, define frame dimensions and pivots, clean transparent edges, and set collision footprints. Preserve crisp rendering; use nearest-neighbor scaling when appropriate.
